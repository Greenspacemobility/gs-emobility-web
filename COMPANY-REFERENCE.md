### Full description (LinkedIn About, Wikipedia, GBP, press boilerplate)
```
Greenspace E-mobility designs, builds and operates high-power EV charging infrastructure powered by on-site solar generation and battery storage. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Panama City, the company develops charging hubs ("Green Hubs") for residential, commercial, public and fleet customers, and is building an electric highway corridor to decarbonize heavy-duty freight between Mexico and Texas. Greenspace operates across Panama, Monterrey (Mexico) and Texas (USA), with a center of excellence in Oslo, Norway. Its integrated model combines high-power DC charging, renewable energy, battery storage and a proprietary charging-management platform — enabling fast, reliable charging even where the grid is constrained.
```
