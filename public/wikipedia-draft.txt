'''Greenspace E-Mobility''' (also known as '''GS E-Mobility''') is a Latin American [[electric vehicle charging infrastructure]] company and [[electric vehicle]] distributor headquartered in [[Panama City]], [[Panama]]. The company builds, installs, and operates high-power [[charging station|EV charging stations]], and distributes [[Autel Energy]] chargers and [[Windrose]] [[Class 8 truck|Class 8 electric trucks]] across [[Panama]], [[Mexico]], the [[United States]], and [[Norway]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gs-emobility.com|title=Greenspace E-Mobility|accessdate=2025}}</ref>

== History ==

Greenspace E-Mobility was founded in 2020 with a focus on establishing [[electric vehicle]] charging infrastructure across [[Central America]] and [[Latin America]], markets that had seen limited penetration of EV charging compared to [[North America]] and [[Europe]] at the time of founding.

The company established its first operations in [[Panama]], where it became an early provider of commercial [[DC fast charging]] infrastructure. It subsequently expanded its geographic footprint to include [[Monterrey]], [[Nuevo León]], Mexico, [[Texas]], United States, and [[Oslo]], Norway.

== Operations ==

=== EV Charging Infrastructure ===

Greenspace E-Mobility designs, supplies, installs, and operates Level 2 AC and [[DC fast charging]] stations for residential, commercial, public, and fleet customers. The company is an official distributor of [[Autel Energy]] MaxiCharger products, which range from 7.2 [[kilowatt|kW]] [[Level 2 charging|Level 2 AC chargers]] to 360 kW ultra-fast [[DC fast charging|DC fast chargers]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gs-emobility.com/en/products|title=Products — Greenspace E-Mobility|accessdate=2025}}</ref>

=== Windrose Electric Truck Distribution ===

Greenspace E-Mobility serves as the exclusive distributor of [[Windrose]] Class 8 electric trucks in [[Latin America]]. The Windrose truck offers up to 500 kilometres (310 mi) of range per charge, a 422 kWh battery, 480 kW of motor power, and 6,000 [[Newton metre|Nm]] of torque, with a gross vehicle weight of up to 36 [[tonne]]s.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gs-emobility.com/en/products#windrose|title=Windrose Electric Truck — Greenspace E-Mobility|accessdate=2025}}</ref>

=== Mexico–Texas Electric Highway ===

Greenspace E-Mobility is developing the '''Greenspace Electric Highway''', a planned network of [[DC fast charging]] stations for commercial freight along [[Mexican Federal Highway 85]] in Mexico and [[Interstate 35]] in the United States, connecting [[Monterrey]] to [[Dallas]] and the [[Texas Triangle]] ([[Dallas–Fort Worth metroplex|Dallas]]–[[Houston]]–[[San Antonio]]).<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.gs-emobility.com/en/electric-highway|title=Electric Highway — Greenspace E-Mobility|accessdate=2025}}</ref>

The project is structured across five phases:
* '''Phase 1''' – Initial hubs along Hwy 85 MX
* '''Phase 2''' – Expansion: 3 additional sites along the Hwy 85 and I-35 corridor
* '''Phase 3''' – Optimization: 3 further hubs with battery arbitrage capabilities
* '''Phase 4''' – Completion: 4 final hubs with [[Megawatt Charging System|Megawatt Charging System (MCS)]] technology
* '''Phase 5''' – Texas Triangle: connecting [[Dallas]] to [[Houston]] and back to [[Laredo, Texas|Laredo]]

The corridor targets the Mexico–United States freight route, through which over [[US$]]800 billion in annual trade flows under the [[United States–Mexico–Canada Agreement|USMCA]] trade agreement.

=== Charging Management Platform ===

The company operates a cloud-based [[OCPP]]-compatible smart charging management platform for network operators, providing real-time monitoring, energy optimization, demand response, and fleet charging scheduling.
