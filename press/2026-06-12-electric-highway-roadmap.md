## Greenspace E-mobility Unveils Four-Market Electric Highway to Decarbonize Freight Across the Americas

### Twelve solar-powered Green Hubs and 100 Class 8 electric trucks will link Texas, Monterrey, California and Panama along the hemisphere's most strategic trade corridors

**PANAMA CITY, Panama — June 12, 2026** — Greenspace E-mobility today announced its Electric Highway, a four-market network of high-power, solar-powered charging hubs designed to make long-haul electric freight viable across the Americas. The plan calls for twelve "Green Hubs" and a deployment of 100 Class 8 electric trucks spanning Texas, Monterrey (Mexico), California and Panama — connecting some of the busiest and most strategic freight corridors on the continent.

The Electric Highway targets a structural gap in the transition to zero-emission trucking: the trucks exist and can run real freight lanes, but there is nowhere to charge them between origin and destination. Greenspace's model closes that gap by pairing each hub with on-site solar generation and battery storage, enabling megawatt-class charging even at remote highway sites where the grid is constrained.

"Electric trucks are ready; the charging network is what's missing," said William Pui Martinez, Founder and CEO of Greenspace E-mobility. "We're building the corridor that lets a zero-emission truck cross a border without stopping for fuel — designed from day one around solar, storage and high-power charging, so we can build where the grid alone could never support it."

The network will roll out in phases, anchored by an initial launch of Green Hubs in Texas along the Laredo–Dallas corridor — the single busiest freight gateway between the United States and Mexico — followed by Monterrey to complete the first fully electric cross-border route, and subsequent expansion into California and Panama. Each hub combines high-power DC charging, renewable generation, battery storage and Greenspace's proprietary charging-management platform.

Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Panama City, Greenspace E-mobility designs, builds and operates EV charging infrastructure across Panama, Mexico, the United States and Norway, serving residential, commercial, public and fleet customers. The Electric Highway extends that work to the heavy-duty segment — an area largely untouched by existing charging networks in the region.

The company is engaging fleet operators, site partners and logistics stakeholders along the corridor. Organizations interested in fleet electrification or hosting a charging hub can contact Greenspace at info@gs-emobility.com.

### About Greenspace E-mobility

Greenspace E-mobility designs, builds and operates high-power EV charging infrastructure powered by on-site solar generation and battery storage. Founded in 2020 and headquartered in Panama City, the company develops charging hubs ("Green Hubs") for residential, commercial, public and fleet customers, and is building an electric highway corridor to decarbonize heavy-duty freight across the Americas. Greenspace operates across Panama, Monterrey (Mexico) and Texas (USA), with a center of excellence in Oslo, Norway. Learn more at https://www.gs-emobility.com.
